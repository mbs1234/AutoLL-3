# AutoLL-3 v1.8.4

Keeps every fix from 1.8.3 for the October 3, 2026 audit, and restores what
those fixes stopped. A review of 1.8.3 found the regressions; each one below has
a test that fails on 1.8.3.

- **A return time changed by hand reaches Disney again.** In 1.8.3, Change →
  pick a time → Modify Lightning Lane stopped with "Action stopped before send".
  A screen opened straight after going back took the previous screen's position
  for its own, believed itself hidden, and 1.8.3 lets no hidden screen send.
  Each screen now gets its position from the navigator.
- **A lost booking or cancel no longer freezes the attraction for the day.**
  1.8.3 protected them, but only a move or swap could clear itself. A booking now
  clears when Plans show any of its guests holding that attraction that day, or
  when Plans read 30 seconds after the request still show nothing; a cancel when
  its passes are gone, or still there 30 seconds on. Disney refuses a repeat, so
  either answer is safe. Moves and swaps still need the exact result.
  _Corrected since: a refused repeat does not make a late request harmless.
  1.8.5 makes a cancel, and 1.9.0 a booking, clear only on proof._
- **A refusal is a refusal.** A 403 or 429 on Autopilot's booking request was
  read as "no answer" and froze the attraction; it paces the retry again
  instead. A booking of Autopilot's own whose answer is lost is held by its
  action ledger, as in 1.8.2, which keeps it from being booked twice until Plans
  settle it; its lost moves and swaps stay protected as in 1.8.3.
- **One capacity rule, over the guests who would be booked.** A linked guest
  who cannot be booked no longer stops a swap, a whole party holding unequal
  passes swaps again, and Whole party only off no longer books part of the
  party when a swap would cover everyone. Dry run shows the swap.
- **Saved guests no longer on the account are named.** Party Selection says so
  and drops them on save; Plan Check's party check warns, since Whole party only
  would otherwise wait for them forever.
- **A Time Search stopped by a party change says so,** releasing its lock and
  wake lock.
- **The booking-response reader requires what the app needs:** the attraction's
  entry, a return window that parses, one booked guest with an entitlement, and
  a party. A Modify for part of the party, an extra entry or a guest without a
  name is read and logged. Anything else unreadable after sending is still an
  unknown outcome.
- **Manual-action messages say what happened.** "Nothing was sent" now tells a
  busy reservation (another AutoLL action, passes within two minutes) from a
  protected one, and full storage from both. A cleanup whose answer was lost
  says it may not have gone through. The screens offer their button again once
  the protection clears, and ask Plans again while they wait.
- **Sign-in:** the script download (15 s) and Disney's setup (30 s) are timed
  separately; a slow setup keeps running, and Retry rejoins it. A download still
  in progress is waited for, not duplicated.
- **Restore recovery:** the notice says once what happened, confirms the export,
  counts failed retries, blocks a second restore, and holds the screen for a
  reload when the original plan is back.
- **Protection wording:** Today's banner and the protection panel say manual
  actions are paused too, and which entries clear by themselves.

Verified locally: 133 suites and 2,115 tests passed in four full runs; no test
ends with a load still showing; lint, TypeScript checking, Prettier and the
production build passed; the dependency audit reported zero vulnerabilities.
In the harness, against fake clients: a hand booking, a one-guest cancel, a
hand Change of return time and an Autopilot booking. No real Disney reservation
was created or modified for these checks, and the booking-response reader has
still not seen a real Disney answer.

Not changed: the inherited runtime module and installer revisions, the backup
format, and what a backup contains (never the sign-in). Known small items are
in [docs/FUTURE.md](../FUTURE.md) §1 and §6.
